Plug Power Reports Continued Business Progress,

SLINGERLANDS, N.Y., October 15, 2026
– Plug Power
Inc. (NASDAQ: PLUG), a global leader in comprehensive hydrogen solutions for the hydrogen economy, is providing an update today on its
2026 business progress, highlighting improvements across three key business priorities: sales growth, margin enhancement, and improved
cash flows. The Company is also sharing details for its upcoming third quarter earnings call scheduled for November 9 and Plug Symposium
2026 scheduled for November 19.
"Throughout 2026, we have remained focused on strengthening Plug's
financial foundation through disciplined execution," said Jose Luis Crespo, Chief Executive Officer of Plug. "Our progress across
sales, margin and cash burn demonstrates that the actions we are taking are delivering results. As we move through the fourth quarter,
we remain focused on improving operating leverage, managing cash and delivering on our commercial commitments while building our pipeline
for 2027 and beyond."
2026 Business Progress and Priorities
Plug continues to execute against its three key business objectives,
with progress through the third quarter and continued focus on year-end performance:
·
Sales:
Based on preliminary, unaudited estimates, Plug expects meaningful
revenue growth in Q3 2026 of greater than 10% versus Q3 2025. Progress on sales and pipeline growth suggest the Company is on track to
achieve its previously communicated full-year revenue growth guidance of approximately 15% to 16%, supported by commercial execution across
material handling, electrolyzers and hydrogen supply.
·
Margin:
The Company has demonstrated gross margin improvement over
the past two quarters and expects continued year-over-year progress in Q3 2026, reflecting the focus on cost reductions and improved operating
leverage.
·
Cash:
Based on preliminary, unaudited estimates, Plug’s net cash usage
for Q3 2026 was approximately $28 million, marking continued improvement for the year. Plug ended Q3 2026 with approximately $134 million
in cash and cash equivalents (excluding restricted cash). The Company did not raise any proceeds through its at-the-market (ATM) offering
program or generate proceeds from other equity or debt capital raises during the quarter. Hence, this net cash usage for the third quarter
reflects overall operating results and fundamental improvement, which the Company believes supports its forecast for continued improvement
in Q4 2026.
Looking ahead, Plug remains focused on sustaining progress across all
three objectives in the fourth quarter and going forward, with an emphasis on disciplined cash management, minimizing capital expenditures,
and strengthening its operating performance.
Pipeline Developments
Plug continues to advance its global GenEco electrolyzer pipeline,
converting commercial opportunities into projects across sustainable fuels, ammonia production and industrial hydrogen applications.
Recent developments include:
·
Arcadia eFuels:
Signed a 280 MW GenEco electrolyzer supply agreement
for Project ENDOR in Denmark, alongside a strategic cooperation agreement positioning Plug as the preferred supplier for more than 1 GW
of potential e-SAF projects across Europe and the Americas.
·
Hy2gen:
Advancing front-end engineering design (FEED) for the 275
MW Courant project in Québec, Canada, supporting renewable ammonia and ammonium nitrate production.
·
Orica:
Secured a 50 MW GenEco electrolyzer order for the Hunter Valley
Hydrogen Hub in Australia, following the project's final investment decision (FID).
·
Iberdrola and Galp:
Continuing execution and commissioning activities
for the 25 MW Iberdrola and bp project in Spain and the 100 MW Galp project in Portugal.
These developments reinforce Plug's approximately $8 billion global
electrolyzer opportunity funnel and demonstrate continued commercial progress across multiple markets. While project timelines vary, particularly
for complex, first-of-a-kind developments, Plug remains focused on advancing opportunities through key milestones and converting its pipeline
into revenue, supporting its near- and mid-term growth objectives.

About Plug Power
Plug is building the
global hydrogen economy with a fully integrated ecosystem spanning production, storage, delivery, and power generation. A first mover
in the industry, Plug provides electrolyzers, liquid hydrogen, fuel cell systems, storage tanks, and fueling infrastructure to customers
worldwide across material handling, industrial applications, and energy production, advancing energy independence and decarbonization
at scale.
Plug has deployed electrolyzers on five continents,
along with more than 76,000 fuel cell systems and 280 fueling stations, and is the world's largest user of liquid hydrogen. Its expanding
generation network has production capacity of 40 tons of hydrogen per day, supplying large-scale projects that redefine industrial power
for customers globally.
Headquartered
in New York, with employees and state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities around the world, Plug powers global leaders like BMW, BP, Walmart,
Amazon, and Home Depot. For more information, visit
www.plugpower.com
.
