Select Recent Revita Clinical Highlights
Fractyl is studying Revita in the REMAIN-1 weight maintenance program, which is designed to evaluate Revita’s potential to maintain weight loss following GLP-1 based therapy discontinuation. The REMAIN-1 program includes three distinct participant cohorts that are conducted under a single IDE: the REVEAL-1 Cohort, the REMAIN-1 Midpoint Cohort and the REMAIN-1 Pivotal Cohort.
•
In October 2026, Fractyl reported positive topline six-month results from the REMAIN-1 Pivotal Cohort, a 308-participant, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled study of Revita for weight maintenance in adults with obesity following discontinuation of GLP-1 therapy. REMAIN-1 met its pre-specified six-month co-primary endpoint of weight loss retention, as patients treated with a single Revita procedure demonstrated a highly statistically significant reduction in weight regain compared to sham (p=0.0023).
o
Six-month co-primary endpoint met:
In the modified intention-to-treat (mITT) population (N=308), Revita participants maintained approximately 80% of their GLP-1-induced weight loss after a single treatment at six months with high statistical significance versus sham (p=0.0023). 32% of Revita
participants were either weight stable or had further weight loss at six months post GLP-1 discontinuation (<2% regain or incremental loss) versus 18% for sham (post hoc; nominal p=0.020).
o
Dose-dependent effect observed:
In a post hoc analysis by ablation length, participants who received 12 to 18 cm of duodenal ablation had 43% less regain than sham (observed mean weight regain of 4.7% versus 8.2%; n=107 versus 74). Participants who received 20 cm or more had 53% less regain than sham (mean regain of 3.8%; n=47).
o
Treatment effect grew over time:
The difference in weight regain between Revita and sham widened at each assessment, nearly doubling from Month 3 to Month 6.
o
Favorable interim safety data:
There have been no device- or procedure-related serious adverse events, no deaths, and no unanticipated adverse device effects observed as of the September 24, 2026, data cut-off date. Related adverse events were predominantly mild and peri-procedural, with 94% occurring within seven days of the procedure.
•
The second co-primary endpoint, the proportion of Revita participants maintaining at least 5% total body weight loss relative to their pre-tirzepatide weight at one year, will be assessed against an FDA-mandated pre-specified performance goal of greater than 50%.
Commercial Readiness and Market Access
•
In September 2026, Fractyl hosted a Commercial Investor Day outlining the potential path to commercializing Revita in post-GLP-1 weight maintenance. The Company’s launch plan focuses on the top 100 to 200 of approximately 1,000 accredited bariatric centers in the United States, beginning with 30 to 50 centers in the first year.
•
In October 2026, the American Medical Association published Category III CPT code 1122T, a dedicated code for the Revita procedure, which takes effect January 1, 2027, well ahead of a potential U.S. launch in early 2028.
•
The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) has proposed RAPID, a pathway that could align Medicare coverage with FDA authorization for breakthrough devices. More than 160 comments were filed during the comment period, nearly all supporting an on-ramp for devices with pivotal trials already underway, including comments from ASGE and ASMBS that cited Revita. Fractyl expects to learn whether CMS adopts such an on-ramp in early January 2027.
•
In October 2026, Fractyl announced findings from company-sponsored market research highlighting strong patient interest in a one-time procedural approach to weight maintenance following GLP-1 therapy. Among nearly 300 current or recent GLP-1 users surveyed, 33% preferred stopping GLP-1 therapy and receiving a hypothetical endoscopic procedure.
•
In October 2026, Fractyl announced additions to its U.S. patent portfolio for Revita. The U.S. Patent and Trademark Office issued U.S. Patent No. 12,740,821, covering features of the Revita single-use catheter, with a term extending to November 28, 2042, and issued a Notice of Allowance for a patent application directed to Revita’s thermal dosing method. Fractyl’s intellectual property portfolio includes 39 granted U.S. patents, approximately 40 pending U.S. applications and more than 50 issued patents outside the United States.
Sharpened Focus on Revita
On October 21, 2026, Fractyl adopted a plan to sharpen its strategic focus on its Revita program in connection with a strategic reprioritization, concentrating its financial and operational resources on advancing Revita toward potential FDA marketing authorization and commercialization in post-GLP-1 weight maintenance.
•
Workforce Reduction:
The Company is reducing its workforce by approximately 45%, with the reduction expected to be completed during the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company estimates that it will incur charges of approximately $2 million for severance and other employee termination-related costs, with the majority of such costs to be incurred in the fourth quarter of 2026.
•
Reduced Quarterly Operating Cash Spending:
As cost reductions take effect and REMAIN-1 Pivotal Cohort expenditures decline, the Company expects quarterly operating cash spending to be reduced by approximately 50%, to approximately $8 million, by the end of Q2 2027.
•
Rejuva Strategic Alternatives:
The Company is discontinuing internal development of its Rejuva gene therapy program and is seeking strategic alternatives to maximize the value of the program. There can be no assurance that the Company’s review of strategic alternatives for Rejuva will result in any transaction, and the Company does not intend to disclose further developments unless and until it determines that disclosure is appropriate or required.
Appointment of Michael Wartell as Independent Director
•
On November 6, 2026, the Company announced that effective November 2, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) increased the size of the Board from nine to ten and appointed Michael Wartell as a Class III director, with a term expiring at the Company’s 2027 annual meeting of stockholders and until his successor is duly elected and qualified or until his earlier resignation or removal. Mr. Wartell is the President of Bluerose Associates LLC and has extensive experience serving as an independent director for both public and private corporations, specializing in guiding companies through complex situations.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•
Research and Development Expenses:
R&D expenses were $12.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $17.5 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease of $5.0 million was primarily related to reduced spending on Fractyl’s Revita and Rejuva programs, as well as lower personnel-related expenses.
•
Selling, General and Administrative Expenses:
SG&A expenses were $6.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.2 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $1.0 million was primarily driven by higher personnel-related expenses and stock compensation expenses, partially offset by decreased professional service fees.
•
Net Loss:
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Fractyl reported a net loss of $14.2 million, compared to a net loss of $45.6 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease in net loss of $31.5 million was driven by a $4.0 million reduction in operating expenses, a $25.7 million favorable non-cash change in the fair value of warrant liabilities, $1.6 million lower non-cash loss from change in fair value of debt, and $0.1 million higher interest income.
•
Adjusted EBITDA:
Adjusted EBITDA was negative $16.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to negative $20.8 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease in the non-GAAP adjusted loss of $4.8 million was primarily due to reduced operating expenses excluding stock compensation expenses.
•
Cash Position:
As of September 30, 2026, Fractyl had approximately $31.5 million in cash and cash equivalents. Based on current business plans, the Company believes its cash position will fund operations into the second quarter of 2027, through the planned De Novo submission and anticipated 12-month REMAIN-1 Pivotal Cohort data, subject to compliance with the minimum liquidity covenant under the Company’s credit agreement, as discussed further in the “Liquidity and Capital Resources” section of our Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q.

About Fractyl Health
Fractyl is a clinical-stage metabolic therapeutics company focused on pioneering novel approaches to treat obesity and type 2 diabetes. The Company’s lead product candidate, Revita, is an endoscopic procedural therapy in pivotal development for post-GLP-1 weight maintenance. Fractyl’s goal is to change the course of metabolic disease, advancing treatment from chronic management toward prevention and reversal of disease. Fractyl is headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts.
About Revita
The Revita
®
DMR System is Fractyl’s lead product candidate, designed to remodel the duodenal lining via a single, minimally invasive endoscopic procedure intended to restore healthy nutrient sensing and signaling disrupted by chronic metabolic disease. By targeting duodenal dysfunction, Revita is designed to address an underlying driver of obesity and enable durable weight maintenance following GLP-1 discontinuation. Revita has received FDA Breakthrough Device Designation for weight maintenance in people with obesity who discontinue GLP-1 therapies. Revita is for investigational use only in the United States and is CE marked in the European Union and United Kingdom.
