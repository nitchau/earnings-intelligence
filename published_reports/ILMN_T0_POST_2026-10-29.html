Institutional Conference Call Analyst Q&A Highlights & Critical Analyst Questions
Dan Arias (Stifel) NovaSeq X Elasticity & Consumable Pull-Through

Question: Can you provide more detail on consumable pull-through per NovaSeq X instrument, and whether clinical customers are transitioning all legacy NovaSeq 6000 volume over?

Executive Answer: Jacob Thaysen (CEO) stated that NovaSeq X placement momentum remains strong with clinical customers driving the majority of high-output runs. Reagent utilization is ramping according to plan, with customers reporting significant sequencing economics improvements.

Puneet Souda (Leerink Partners) China Regulatory Scrutiny & Unreliable Entities List

Question: What is the latest status regarding the Chinese Ministry of Commerce regulatory inquiries, and how much China risk is baked into the raised FY26 guidance?

Executive Answer: Ankur Dhingra (CFO) noted that China revenue was down mid-single digits, which is fully factored into the updated guidance. The ROW (Rest of World) organic growth target of >5% reflects strong clinical adoption across the US, Europe, and Japan offsetting China headwinds.

Rachel Vatnsdal (JPMorgan) Research & Academic Budget Pressures vs Clinical Resilience

Question: Are you seeing any worsening in academic and government funding delays in the US and Europe, and how is that impacting instrument purchasing cycles?

Executive Answer: Jacob Thaysen explained that while academic purchasing cycles remain extended with elongated approval processes, clinical oncology and rare disease testing now represent over 50% of total revenue and continue to grow at double-digit rates, providing high revenue visibility.
