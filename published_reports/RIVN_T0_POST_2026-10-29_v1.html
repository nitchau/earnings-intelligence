Institutional Conference Call Analyst Q&A Highlights & Critical Analyst Questions
Adam Jonas (Morgan Stanley) R2 Ramp Velocity, Unit Economics & Cannibalization

Question: Can you provide more color on the customer profile for the R2? Are you seeing any cannibalization of R1S demand, and what are the gross margins on R2 relative to the corporate average?

Executive Answer: RJ Scaringe (CEO) emphasized that R2 is bringing overwhelmingly new buyers into the Rivian brand, with over 70% of purchasers trading in internal combustion vehicles. While Q3 absorbed $120M in launch costs, R2's simplified zonal architecture and structural battery pack position it for double-digit positive gross margins as line rates mature.

Colin Langan (Wells Fargo) Volkswagen Joint Venture Capital Timing & Tech Milestones

Question: How quickly will the cash infusions from the Volkswagen joint venture hit the balance sheet, and when will the shared software stack deploy in production vehicles?

Executive Answer: Claire McDonough (CFO) confirmed that regulatory milestones remain on track, with the first $1B convertible note already closed and subsequent equity tranches scheduled upon joint venture formation. Engineering teams are already co-developing the next-generation zonal compute platform.

George Gianarikas (Canaccord Genuity) Georgia Fab Construction Timeline & Department of Energy Loan

Question: What is the update on resuming construction at the Georgia manufacturing facility, and what is the status of the DOE ATVM loan application?

Executive Answer: RJ Scaringe explained that pausing Georgia to launch R2 in Normal saved over $2.25B in near-term capital. The DOE loan due diligence is progressing positively, and resumption of Georgia construction will be timed to align with R2 volume expansion and R3 international launch in late 2027.
