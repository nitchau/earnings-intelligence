Michael Ryskin (Bank of America) MRD Ramp, Reimbursement Coverage & Diagnostics Margins

Question: Jim, with MRD tests reaching 12,000 units and gross margins expanding to 64.8%, what is your timeline for broad Medicare and commercial coverage for pan-cancer recurrence monitoring?

He highlighted that lab automation is driving significant cost-of-sequencing efficiencies, allowing gross margins to scale toward 68%-70% over the next 18 months.

Dan Leonard (UBS) Cash Runway, Path to Positive FCF & CapEx Discipline

Question: With $854M in cash and marketable securities and Q3 Adjusted EBITDA hitting $25.6M, when do you anticipate crossing into positive quarterly Free Cash Flow after CapEx and lease commitments?

Executive Answer: Jim Rogers (CFO) stated that Tempus expects operating cash burn to continue contracting sequentially, targeting sustained positive operating cash flow by mid-2027. He emphasized that the $854M balance sheet provides ample runway without any need for external dilutive equity financing.
