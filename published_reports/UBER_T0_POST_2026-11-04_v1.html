4. Operational Wins vs Drags & Call Q&A Forensic Audit

Operational Tailwinds & Strategic Wins

  • Gross Bookings Acceleration: Fifth consecutive quarter of 20%+ Gross Bookings growth, climbing to $59.82B with Delivery accelerating +22% YoY.
  • Trip Frequency Records: Total trips expanded 16% YoY to 4.06 billion, powered by 215 million Monthly Active Platform Consumers (MAPCs).
  • EBITDA Margin High: Adjusted EBITDA reached a record $2.96B (5.0% of Gross Bookings), reflecting disciplined marketplace matching and incentive optimization.
  • Autonomous Leadership: Scaling to 700 fully driverless robotaxis on the platform by end-2026 and >2,000 in 2027 through Waymo, Cruise, and WeRide partnerships.

Execution Headwinds & Operational Drags

  • Non-GAAP EPS Narrow Miss: Reported $0.88 vs $0.90 Street consensus (-$0.02 delta), driven by targeted delivery rider supply investments in suburban metros.
  • Reported Mobility Revenue Contraction: Mobility GAAP revenue declined -2% YoY to $7,520M due to business model presentation shifts (8% headwind).
  • Equity Portfolio Revaluation Mark: GAAP net income absorbed a -$476M pre-tax net loss from mark-to-market revaluations on strategic minority holdings.
  • Foreign Exchange Drag: Management highlighted roughly 1 percentage point of constant-currency currency friction entering the Q4 holiday season.

Top Institutional Analyst Q&A and Analyst Questions Forensic Exchanges

Brian Nowak (Morgan Stanley) Autonomous Vehicle Fleet Economics & Waymo / Cruise Commercialization

Question: Can you provide more detail on the economics of autonomous trips as you scale from 700 vehicles at year-end to over 2,000 in 2027? How do Uber's unit economics compare between human drivers and AV fleet partners?

Executive Answer: Dara Khosrowshahi emphasized that Uber provides the highest-density demand and dispatch engine in the world, allowing AV partners to maximize vehicle asset utilization. In early commercial markets like Phoenix and Austin, AV trip completion rates and customer satisfaction are at record highs. Over time, AVs will lower trip costs while expanding Uber's marketplace take rate.

Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) Delivery Take Rate Expansion & Advertising Run-Rate Inflection

Question: Delivery segment operating income expanded 30% YoY to over $1.05B. How much of this profitability is being driven by advertising revenue, and what is your current visibility on advertising reaching your $1.5B target?

Executive Answer: Balaji Krishnamurthy highlighted that advertising revenue continues to grow well over 40% YoY, benefiting from sponsored restaurant listings and CPG brand ad units in grocery. Advertising now accounts for over 1.5% of Gross Bookings, providing direct margin accretion that flows almost entirely into segment operating income.

Justin Post (Bank of America) Capital Allocation, Share Repurchases & Free Cash Flow Conversion

Question: With free cash flow reaching $2.54B in the quarter and total liquidity at $10.1B, what is the pace of share repurchases going forward, and will you consider initiating a recurring dividend?

Executive Answer: Balaji Krishnamurthy affirmed that share repurchases remain the primary vehicle for capital return, consistently neutralizing stock-based compensation dilution while shrinking share count. Management maintains a disciplined hurdle for organic investment and will evaluate capital return expansions as FCF exceeds $10B annualized.
