Sanjit Singh (Morgan Stanley) AI Workload Monitoring Monetization & Customer Usage Trajectory

We see AI accelerating cloud data consumption across all three pillars.'

Brad Zelnick (Deutsche Bank) Gross Margin Sustainability & Infrastructure Efficiency

We've optimized our multi-cloud architecture and tiering of log data. While we continue to reinvest in new AI capabilities, we feel very comfortable with gross margins remaining in the low-to-mid 80s range.'

Kash Rangan (Goldman Sachs) Platform Consolidation & Multi-Product Cross-Selling Momentum

Executive Answer: Olivier Pomel explained: 'Consolidation is our primary growth engine. CIOs are actively eliminating separate vendors for APM, infrastructure, logs, and cloud security. Over 49% of our customers now use four or more products, and our security products continue to show the fastest adoption curves in our history. The Datadog unified data model is an unbeatable value proposition.'
