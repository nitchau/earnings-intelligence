Brian Peterson (Raymond James) Crossing the $1B Run-Rate & Enterprise Accounts Spending >$50K

Question: Sanjeev, crossing the $1B run rate is a huge milestone. Can you talk about the 26% growth in $50K+ accounts, and how enterprise procurement integrations like TradeCentric are driving larger share of wallet?

Executive Answer: Sanjeev Singh Sahni (CEO) stated: 'Crossing the $1 billion revenue run rate reflects our transformation from a spot quoting tool into essential enterprise supply chain infrastructure. Eric Sheridan (Goldman Sachs) AI Sourcing Models, Workcenter Launch & Marketplace Gross Margin

Question: James, marketplace gross margin hit a record 36.0%. How much of that expansion is driven by your new AI pricing algorithms versus supplier network scale, and what is the sustainable margin ceiling?

Executive Answer: James Miln (CFO) responded: 'Our machine learning models continuously ingest supplier capacity, material spot costs, and regional delivery metrics. The 30 bps expansion to 36.0% was directly driven by improved pricing accuracy and automated routing in our new Workcenter portal. While we maintain a long-term target of 35-36%, our growing scale gives us exceptional pricing power and supplier engagement.'

Shawn Harrison (Loop Capital) Aerospace, Defense & Texas Manufacturing Hub Velocity

Question: Sanjeev, you announced a partnership with MxD for CMMC cybersecurity certification and noted Texas partner jobs grew twice the national rate. How is defense and aerospace demand trending given reshoring mandates?

Executive Answer: Sanjeev Singh Sahni explained: 'Defense and aerospace are among our fastest-growing sectors. Prime contractors require ITAR-compliant, CMMC-certified domestic suppliers, and our AI marketplace instantly identifies qualified American machine shops. Our Texas supplier network is expanding rapidly to meet aerospace demand, proving that digital manufacturing is central to the reshoring of the American industrial base.'
