Institutional Conference Call Analyst Questions & Executive Answers (Analyst Q&A)
Tien-tsin Huang (JPMorgan) Project Elevate Savings Expansion to $1B & Goodwill Impairment Trigger

Question: Takis, you doubled the Project Elevate target to $1 billion while taking a $6.45B non-cash goodwill impairment. How much of the $1B savings flows through to net operating income in 2027 versus being reinvested, and what drove the timing of the impairment?

Executive Answer: Takis Georgakopoulos (CEO) replied: 'The goodwill impairment was a non-cash accounting exercise required by the sustained pressure on our equity valuation; it has zero impact on our cash balances, bank covenants, or liquidity. Regarding Project Elevate, we identified profound structural efficiencies as we apply AI across customer service, code modernization, and merchant onboarding. We expect at least half of the $1 billion in annualized savings to drop directly to operating income over the next 18 to 24 months.'

Jason Kupferberg (Bank of America) Clover Volume Growth & Merchant Attrition vs Integrated FinTech Rivals

Question: Paul, Merchant Solutions revenue was down 3% GAAP and 2% organic. Can you unpack Clover's performance, merchant take rates, and how competitive dynamics are playing out against Toast and Square?

Executive Answer: Paul Todd (CFO) answered: 'Clover volume continues to grow at double-digit rates, and software attach is expanding. The slight headline revenue decline was primarily driven by lower hardware shipments compared to tough comps and our deliberate pruning of low-margin processing volume. As our modern merchant platform ramps, we see merchant organic revenue re-accelerating in the fourth quarter.'

Ramsey El-Assal (Barclays) Banking Attrition Reduction & Q4 Revenue Acceleration Path

Question: Takis, net attrition in banking is down 25% YTD and Financial Solutions was flat GAAP / +2% organic. What gives you confidence in sequential acceleration for Q4, and are you seeing core conversion wins?

Executive Answer: Takis Georgakopoulos explained: 'Client retention is our top operating priority, and seeing net banking attrition fall over 25% is proof that our One Fiserv integration is working. We signed multiple major financial institution renewals in Q3 that start billing in Q4. That gives us full line of sight to achieving our full-year organic guidance and returning to sustainable growth in 2027.'
