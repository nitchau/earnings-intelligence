Institutional Conference Call Analyst Q&A Highlights & Critical Analyst Questions
Kenneth Worthington (JPMorgan) USDC Yield Sharing Dynamics & Regulatory Scrutiny

Question: Can you elaborate on how potential Fed rate cuts will impact the stablecoin revenue sharing economics with Circle, and what percentage of USDC is migrating off-platform?

Executive Answer: Alesia Haas (CFO) emphasized that while rate cuts compress gross yield, the total supply of USDC in Coinbase products reached a new all-time high in Q3. Platform stickiness and international adoption via Base and institutional custody are offsetting rate headwinds.

Dan Dolev (Mizuho) Derivatives Market Share & Deribit Unified Exchange

Question: How quickly can Coinbase convert international derivatives volume into revenue, and what is the cannibalization risk to existing spot trading fees?

Executive Answer: Brian Armstrong (CEO) stated that derivatives trading volume grew 179% CAGR over the last two years and reached an all-time high market share of 10.7%. Relaunching Coinbase Pro with unified cross-collateralization allows traders to use spot balances as margin, increasing net platform activity.

Devin Ryan (Citizens JMP) Prediction Markets & Base Layer-2 Monetization

Question: What contributed to the surge in prediction market trading volume this quarter, and how does sequencer revenue on Base fit into medium-term revenue?

Executive Answer: Emilie Choi (COO) noted that election and macro prediction contracts provided an important non-correlated volume cushion during low crypto volatility. Base sequencer fees are currently optimized for developer low-cost transactions, but app ecosystem traction is creating long-term subscription value.
