3. Silicon Photonics Integration (FMCW 4D Advantage vs. Time-of-Flight) PROPRIETARY SILICON

Unlike legacy pulsed Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors susceptible to sunlight blooming and multi-sensor crosstalk, Aeva's proprietary Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) architecture directly measures instantaneous velocity (Doppler effect) per pixel alongside 3D spatial coordinates. By integrating lasers, detectors, and optical processing into a single silicon photonics chip, Aeva reduces bill-of-materials cost toward commercial serial production targets.
