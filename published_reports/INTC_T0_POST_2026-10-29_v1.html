Institutional Conference Call Analyst Q&A Highlights & Critical Analyst Questions
C.J. Muse (Cantor Fitzgerald) Q4 Gross Margin Compression & Lunar Lake Tile Cost Structure

Question: Why is Q4 gross margin stepping down 500 bps from 43% to 38% despite higher full-year revenue volumes, and how much of that is driven by external TSMC packaging costs on Lunar Lake?

Executive Answer: David Zinsner (CFO) admitted that Lunar Lake’s architecture relies on external foundry packaging which carries lower initial gross margins until Panther Lake brings high-volume compute tiles back to internal 18A fabs in 2027. Q4 also bears elevated start-up costs for Fab 34 and Fab 52.

Timothy Arcuri (UBS) Client PC Inventory Digestion & AI PC Momentum

Question: Can you unpack the Client Computing guidance drop to $15B-$16B? Are enterprise customers delaying corporate desktop refreshes until Windows 10 end-of-life in late 2027?

Executive Answer: Pat Gelsinger (CEO) explained that after aggressive pre-ordering in Q3, OEM customers in North America and EMEA are maintaining lean inventory. While AI PC adoption is expanding rapidly with over 100M Core Ultra units targeted by year-end, component supply chains remain disciplined.

Stacy Rasgon (Bernstein Research) Intel Foundry External Commitments & 18A Milestones

Question: Where do customer tape-outs stand on 18A, and how much external cash burn should we expect from Intel Foundry before breakeven in 2028?

Executive Answer: Pat Gelsinger reiterated that 18A is on track for full manufacturing readiness, with Panther Lake and Clearwater Forest leading internal production. Commercial foundry pipeline stands at over $15B in lifetime deal value, with major hyperscaler tape-outs scheduled for H1 2027.
