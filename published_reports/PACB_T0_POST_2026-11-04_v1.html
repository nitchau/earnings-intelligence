Subbu Nambi (Guggenheim) Cash Burn Trajectory & Path to 2028 Breakeven

Question: With cash and investments at $199.4M and quarterly operating burn at $34.9M, can you walk us through the cadence of expense reductions that get PacBio to cash flow breakeven without external financing?

Executive Answer: Christian Henry responded: 'We have executed structural restructuring that eliminated over $75M in annualized operating costs, reflected in our OpEx coming down to $50.5M this quarter. As Vega shipments scale and gross margins expand toward 40% with higher consumable attach, our quarterly cash burn will decline sequentially toward -$20M in 2027 and reach breakeven by 2028. We are fully focused on managing our $199.4M liquidity fortress.'

Kyle Mikson (Canaccord Genuity) CFO Transition & Executive Stability

Question: Christian, regarding the announcement that Jim Gibson is stepping down as CFO to take an advisory role, can you give context on the transition timing and what qualifications you are prioritizing in the next finance chief?

Executive Answer: Christian Henry stated: 'Jim has been a terrific partner through our restructuring and asset rationalization. With our major cost-cutting programs implemented and the company on a stabilized operational footing, Jim and the board felt this was the natural time to transition. Jim will remain actively advising through March 2027, ensuring a seamless handover while we conduct a comprehensive search for our next commercial-phase CFO.'

Matt Sykes (Goldman Sachs) SPRQ-Nx Chemistry Adoption & Sequencing Cost Curve

Question: Christian, with the commercial introduction of SPRQ chemistry, how are your high-throughput Revio customers reacting to the lower price per genome, and is that driving elasticity in new customer segments?

Executive Answer: Christian Henry responded: 'SPRQ chemistry has been enthusiastically received because it lowers consumable run costs while boosting Q30+ data accuracy. We are seeing immediate elasticity as existing accounts ramp sample cohorts that were previously cost-prohibitive. That directly drove our $20.8M consumable delivery this quarter and expands our competitive moat against short-read alternatives.'
