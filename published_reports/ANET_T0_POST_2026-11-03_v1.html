Institutional Conference Call Analyst Questions & Executive Answers (Analyst Q&A)
Meta Marshall (Morgan Stanley) Etherlink SU-144 Scale-Up Adoption & Ultra Ethernet Momentum vs InfiniBand

Question: Jayshree, the announcement of Etherlink SU-144 with AMD, Meta, Microsoft, and Broadcom is a major milestone for open scale-up fabrics. Can you talk about how quickly hyperscalers are transitioning from proprietary InfiniBand to Arista Ethernet for their largest generative AI clusters?

Executive Answer: Jayshree Ullal (Chairperson & CEO) stated: 'The shift toward open Ethernet for both scale-up and scale-out AI is happening faster than anticipated. Customers refuse to be locked into proprietary single-vendor stacks. With our Etherlink SU-144 architecture supporting 144 accelerators per rack in a single hop and lossless transport, hyperscalers are realizing equivalent or superior job completion times at significantly lower total cost of ownership. We see Ethernet capturing the vast majority of new AI cluster deployments.'

Samik Chatterjee (JPMorgan) Q4 Operating Margin Durability & Supply Chain Allocation for 800G/1.6T Optics

Question: Chantelle, your Q4 guide of 48-49% operating margin follows this quarter's record 49.1%. As you ramp 1.6T systems and liquid-cooled gear, how are you managing component availability, optical transceivers, and gross margin durability?

Executive Answer: Chantelle Breithaupt (CFO) responded: 'Our gross margin of 64% reflects excellent manufacturing execution and strong software attach. While 1.6T optics and high-radix switch silicon require disciplined inventory staging—which is why inventory grew to $3.2B—our supply chain commitments are fully secured. The 48-49% operating margin guide shows that our operating leverage is structural, not a one-quarter anomaly.'

Tal Liani (Bank of America) Enterprise & Campus Networking Momentum vs Cloud Titan Concentration

Question: Jayshree, product revenue grew 66.8% YoY, clearly driven by cloud titans. What are you seeing in enterprise campus switching and routing, and how is the pipeline shaping up for non-cloud customers deploying private AI models?

Executive Answer: Jayshree Ullal explained: 'While Cloud Titans represented the largest volume driver this quarter, Enterprise and Campus grew double-digits. Enterprises are beginning to deploy private AI clusters on-premise, and they want the exact same Arista EOS software that runs the world's largest AI supercomputers. That gives Arista an unrivaled competitive moat across client-to-cloud.'
