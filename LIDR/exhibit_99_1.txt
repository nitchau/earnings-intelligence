AEye Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Revenue
Increases Approximately 10x
Year-Over-Year and 2.5x Sequentially, Reflecting Accelerating Commercial Momentum
Fourth Quarter Revenue Expected to Double Quarter-Over-Quarter
to Approximately $1 Million;
Establishes New Expected Quarterly Baseline Revenue with Significant Upside Potential in 2027
Customer Base Expands to 32; First STRATOS™
Revenue Recorded
Lunar Outpost Program Begins Contributing Revenue;
Lead Defense Customer Places Fifth Order;
Expanded Entry into Sports Analytics with Second Customer
PLEASANTON, Calif. – November 5, 2026 – AEye, Inc. (Nasdaq:
LIDR), a global leader in software-defined, high-performance lidar solutions, today announced financial results for the third quarter
ended September 30, 2026.
Business Highlights
●
Expanding Commercial Customer Base:
The number of
customers that have taken revenue-generating shipments increased to 32, up 28% from the 25 reported in conjunction with second quarter
2026 results. Active technical engagements are now greater than 200, each one a candidate for a paid proof of concept.
●
Repeat Defense Orders and Growing Engagements:
AEye’s lead defense customer has now placed its fifth order, extending
a pattern of repeat purchasing. Defense remains the Company’s most active vertical and continues to grow quarter-over-quarter, as
Apollo™ and STRATOS™ are well suited for this market.
●
Initial STRATOS™ Revenue:
Recorded first STRATOS™ revenue resulting from engagement with Swiftstrike AI on the
development and integration of STRATOS™ into counter-UAS systems.
●
Lunar Outpost Revenue Contribution:
AEye began recognizing revenue under its binding, multi-million-dollar agreement with Lunar
Outpost, which selected Apollo™ for its Pegasus Lunar Terrain Vehicle. The program engages AEye from design and qualification through
delivery of flight hardware, broadening AEye’s participation in customer programs beyond standard product shipments.
●
Second Customer in Sports Analytics:
Following Alive3D’s selection of Apollo™, a second company has placed an order
for Apollo™ for next-generation sports analytics, applying AEye’s configurable sensing platform to 3D spatial visualization,
precision measurement, and advanced analysis of sports performance.
Management Commentary
“Our success with leveraging our software-defined architecture
into new commercial agreements across a wider range of addressable markets is reflected in our accelerating revenue growth,” said
Matt Fisch, CEO of AEye. “A growing number of customers are choosing AEye for demanding applications that require long-range detection,
rugged hardware, and the ability to adapt sensing behavior as their requirements evolve. Our differentiated architecture lets us address
these needs across different markets without having to start over for each new application, which also helps us operate more efficiently,
and increasingly with less capital required. This key differentiator for AEye was evident in the third quarter as our product flexibility
supported initial revenue contributions from lunar infrastructure development, continued defense orders, and our first revenue from STRATOS™.”
Fisch continued, “We have a growing number of qualified
leads entering our pipeline, a record number of customers integrating and validating our software-defined architecture, and we are
translating this into a growing number of new commercial engagements. We expect to continue converting our record pipeline into
sustained commercial activity, leading to significant revenue growth anticipated in 2027. Our visibility into this expected revenue
ramp includes a combination of repeat product purchases, commercial programs, and new opportunities, supported by a go-to-market
strategy designed to serve a growing number of high-growth markets.”
Financial Highlights
●
Q3 2026 revenue was $506 thousand, approximately 10 times the $50 thousand reported in Q3 2025 and approximately 2.5 times Q2 2026,
representing the Company’s fifth consecutive quarter of revenue growth.
●
Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $809 thousand, compared with $136 thousand in the same period of 2025, nearly six times
higher and approximately 3.5 times full-year 2025 revenue.
●
Gross margin in Q3 2026 improved to (35)% from (80)% in Q2 2026.
●
GAAP net loss in Q3 2026 was $(11.2) million, or $(0.24) per share.
●
Non-GAAP net loss in Q3 2026 was $(7.2) million, or $(0.15) per share.
●
Cash consumption, net of financing costs, in Q3 2026 was $8.3 million.
●
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $63.1 million as of September 30, 2026.
“Revenue grew approximately 10x year-over-year and 2.5x sequentially,
marking AEye’s fifth consecutive quarter of sequential growth,” said Conor Tierney, CFO of AEye. “With Lunar Outpost
ramping alongside repeat sensor orders, we expect the fourth quarter to be our first million dollar revenue quarter driven mainly by Apollo™
commercial programs, establishing a new expected quarterly baseline as we build toward 2027.”
“Gross margin improved 45 percentage points sequentially,
from negative 80% to negative 35%, reflecting a richer revenue mix, lower product costs as our bill of materials comes down, and
lower warranty reserves on improved Apollo™ return rates. We are targeting an approximately 15% reduction in cash consumption
in 2027, and with $63.1 million in cash and marketable securities and a virtually debt free balance sheet, we believe we have the
capacity to support our customers’ programs through the ramp.”
2026 Financial Outlook
With the Lunar Outpost program now contributing and ramping, AEye has visibility to begin providing revenue guidance. For the fourth quarter
2026, revenue is expected to approximately double third quarter 2026 revenue and be the Company’s first $1 million revenue quarter,
driven mainly by Apollo™ commercial programs.
The Company expects that cash consumption for the full year 2026 will
be $35 million, inclusive of approximately $5 million in working capital to support the commercial ramp. The Company is aiming to lower
2027 cash consumption by approximately 15% from the 2026 expected cash consumption level. Under its current operating plan, AEye expects
its available cash resources to support operations and the execution of multi-year commercial programs into 2028.

About AEye
AEye offers a suite of unique software-defined lidar solutions that
address a wide range of real-world needs including advanced driver-assistance, vehicle autonomy, smart infrastructure, security, defense,
and logistics applications. AEye’s flagship product, Apollo™, has been widely recognized for its small form factor and its
ability to detect objects at up to one kilometer. In addition to Apollo™, AEye also offers STRATOS™ with the ability to detect
objects at up to one-and-a-half kilometers as well as a full-stack solution through its OPTIS™ platform. OPTIS™ provides a
complete system that captures a high-resolution 3D image of the world, interprets it, and provides direction to act upon what it sees
in real-time.
